We are Almond

We work with government and semi-government entities and private companies to build initiatives that bring together community insight, creativity, planning, partnerships, delivery and measurement.

We start with the objective, find the opportunity, develop the concept and build the initiative’s journey so it reaches its audience and achieves the intended outcome.

What we work toward

To help evolve initiatives in the Saudi market so they are more connected to society and better able to create measurable, lasting value.

Our methodology

A clear journey.
From purpose to impact.

Seven connected stages, adapted to each initiative’s scale and needs.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the objective, context and audience.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Identify the opportunity the initiative can be built on.

  3. 03

    Create

    Develop the core idea and concept.

  4. 04

    Design

    Build the experience and participation journeys.

  5. 05

    Connect

    Identify partners, channels and opportunities.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Lead operations, marketing and management.

  7. 07

    Measure

    Analyze results and identify opportunities for growth.

Different expertises and One direction.

Creativity, planning and operations working together, from the first concept to the details of delivery.
