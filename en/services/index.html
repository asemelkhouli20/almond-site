Before we begin

Do you only work with companies?

We work with private companies and government and semi-government entities, building partnerships around the needs of each initiative.

Can you develop an existing initiative?

Yes. We start with its objectives and current experience, then identify opportunities to improve the concept, participation, operations and measurement.

How do we get started?

Use the enquiry form to share your organization, objective and project type. We’ll discuss the need and define an appropriate scope together.

How do you choose an initiative concept?

We start with the organization’s objective, audience needs and sector context, then develop the concept, participation experience and delivery plan.

How do you measure initiative performance?

We define success around the objective, considering reach, participation, engagement and value for beneficiaries and partners, then use the findings to guide the next stage.
